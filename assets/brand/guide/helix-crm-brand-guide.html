Proportion

Neutrals carry the layout. Primary appears once per view as a single confident block; accent is a detail, never a background.

Contrast

Ratios above are measured against the better of white or near-black. Anything under 4.5:1 is reserved for large type and graphic shapes.

Backgrounds

Flat fills only — no gradients, no tinted photography washes. Tints are the primary mixed into the neutral at 8% or less.

Marks & Surfaces

Logo & Style

Bold & Geometric

Hard edges with an offset "sticker" shadow in the accent color. Confident and energetic — fits creative, youth or event brands.

Corner language

Radius 0px for controls and cards, 0px for panels and full-bleed blocks.

04/05
How We Sound

Voice

Design is the silent ambassador of your brand.
Paul Rand
We sound like
  • Direct — the point comes first, the detail second.
  • Specific — real numbers and real examples over adjectives.
  • Warm — plain language, written to a person, not a segment.
We never sound like
  • Hype — no superlatives we cannot demonstrate.
  • Jargon — if a shorter word works, it wins.
  • Filler — nothing on the page that does no work.
