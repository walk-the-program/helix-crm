# Brand foundation review — revision 3

Date: 2026-09-19. Owner: design agent (brand foundation).
Direction: `docs/DESIGN.md` revision 3. Values: `src/styles/tokens.css`.
Source: `assets/brand/guide/helix-crm-brand-guide.html`.

What this file records: what the screenshots caught when the component kit was
put next to the brand guide, what was changed because of it, and the evidence
that the result holds.

## How it was checked

The gallery (`design/ui-screens/gallery.html`, regenerated by
`tests/unit/ui/gallery.test.ts`) was served alongside the built app's CSS and
its `/fonts/` and `/helix-logo.png` assets, then captured headless at 1280 via
the pinned Playwright CLI in three combinations, plus fourteen sections in both
themes:

- `gallery-1280-light.png`, `gallery-1280-dark.png`,
  `gallery-1280-light-compact.png`
- `sections/{light,dark}-{brand,button,badgetones,nav,pageheader,table,card,input,field,emptystate,dialog,kbd,sectionlabel,groupedlist}.png`

The brand guide's own five pages were captured the same way for side-by-side
comparison (`design/brand/guide/`).

`design/contrast-audit.js` was run against the rendered gallery in all four
theme × density combinations. It walks every element that paints text, resolves
the real background by climbing to the first opaque ancestor, and applies the
WCAG threshold for the rendered size and weight.

## What the comparison caught, and what changed

**1. Tertiary ink failed on the sidebar tint (fixed).** The first pass set
`--color-text-faint` to `#6A7278`, which measures 4.89:1 on white — the number
you get if you only check against white. The audit found four real failures at
**4.45:1**: three `kbd` glyphs and the "Pinned views" sidebar group label, all
sitting on `#F2F4FA`. The token was darkened to `#646B71`, whose worst pair in
the whole product is 4.61:1 on the hover tint. The alternative — a rule saying
"tertiary ink is not allowed on a tint" — was rejected because nobody would
remember it. The ramp is now compressed (7.58 / 6.11 / 5.41 on white) because
the brand's neutral dark is already a mid grey; size and weight carry the
hierarchy, which is what §4 of the direction already said.

**2. White on the primary was never an option.** `#FFFFFF` on `#97B1C3`
measures **2.24:1**. The brand guide's own cover page sets `#141414` on the
primary, which measures **8.24:1** — the figure printed on its palette page.
That is `--color-accent-text`, and it is the same in both themes.

**3. The secondary cannot carry link text.** `#8B85C2` measures 3.37:1 on
white. It clears the 3:1 bar for a non-text focus ring and nothing else, so the
ring is the pure secondary and `--color-link` is `#5F58A6` — the same hue
darkened to 6.12:1. This is the guide's own sub-4.5:1 rule applied rather than
waved through.

**4. The selected sidebar row had to become the block, and nothing else.** The
guide allows the primary once per view. The first instinct was to point
`--color-selected` at the primary, which would have painted every highlighted
menu item, palette row and selected table row as a block — several per view.
`--color-selected` stayed a quiet tint and `NavItem` paints `--color-accent`
directly, so there is exactly one block and it is always in the same place.
`tests/unit/ui/nav.test.ts` asserts an active row never carries
`--color-selected`.

**5. The sticker shadow needed a surface and needed room.** The mark is a
transparent PNG; with no fill behind it the accent offset reads as a smear
behind the glyphs rather than as an offset card. `Brand` puts it on
`--color-surface` with a hairline, which is also how the guide's "Marks &
Surfaces" page draws it. The sidebar's brand slot gained bottom padding so the
4px overhang is not clipped by the first nav group.

**6. Compact needed the sticker to come down too.** At compact density a 4px
offset behind a 26px mark was proportionally heavier than at comfortable, so
`--shadow-sticker` shortens to `3px 3px 0` under `[data-density="compact"]`.

**7. Two leadings, not one.** The guide sets body at 15/1.65. Applied to `body`
that pushes a label off the centre of a 32px control and off the baseline of a
40px row. `--leading-body` (1.65) is on prose — `p`, descriptions, empty-state
copy — and `--leading-normal` (1.5) stays on rows and controls.

## What the screenshots confirmed

- The lockup reads as the guide's "bold and geometric": hard square, hairline,
  accent offset down and right, wordmark in Zilla Slab bold.
- `PageHeader` at 32/1.1 in the slab is recognisably the guide's "Section
  heading" specimen.
- Badges are rectangles. Every `--radius-*` resolves to 0, so the whole kit
  went square through the tokens without a single component edit.
- The sidebar's one block is identical in light and dark, because the primary
  does not invert.
- Floating layers (dialog, menu, popover, tooltip, palette) are lifted by a
  second hairline with no blur, so nothing casts a grey haze over the content
  it covers.
- The table header is the caption step in Lato; the selected table row is
  the quiet tint, not the block.

## Known and deliberate

- **The ink ramp is compressed.** Three steps between 7.58:1 and 5.41:1 on
  white. A wider ramp would mean either a darker body ink than the brand's
  neutral dark or a tertiary ink that fails on a tint. Neither is worth it.
- **The stage ramp is not brand colour.** A pipeline needs eight separable
  hues; a five-colour palette cannot supply them. The eight pastels are held in
  the same washed-out family so they sit under the brand rather than fight it,
  and the guide is silent on the subject.
- **Danger, success and warning are not brand colour either**, for the same
  reason: they mean something on their own, and the guide does not cover state.
  Info now follows the brand primary.
- **`--radius-full` is 0.** The name is a holdover and every call site still
  compiles; `tests/unit/ui/badge.test.ts` records that the token is 0 on
  purpose so nobody "fixes" the class later.
- **The scrollbar thumb is square**, which follows from the same token. It is
  consistent with the corner language rather than an oversight.
